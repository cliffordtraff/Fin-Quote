AAPL Snapshot

Revenue Outpaces Profit — Gap Widens Since 2019

Apple’s revenue rises from $260.17B in 2019 to $416.16B in 2025 while net income climbs from $55.26B to $112.01B, expanding the absolute gap between top line and bottom line. Profitability improves too: gross margin increases to 46.91% and operating margin reaches 31.97%, and net income posted a big jump after 2020 with net income rising from $57.41B in 2020 to $94.68B in 2021.

Chart shows revenue versus net income for 2019–2025, highlighting the widening dollar gap and improving margins after 2020.

Gross Margin Climbs From 38% to 47%

Apple’s gross margin rises steadily from 37.82% in 2019 to 46.91% in 2025, reflecting improved pricing power and cost mix; the biggest jump came between 2020 (38.23%) and 2021 (41.78%). Revenue in 2025 is $416.16B and net income is $112.01B, underscoring stronger profitability alongside the margin expansion.

Chart shows Apple’s gross margin trend by year, rising from ~37.8% in 2019 to 46.91% in 2025.

Big Cash Engine With Noticeable Yearly Swings

Apple is generating material free cash flow, with FCF rising from $58.9B in 2019 to a peak of $111.4B in 2022 before landing at $98.8B in 2025; the latest year shows $98,767,000,000 supporting buybacks and dividends. Year-to-year volatility is clear—FCF climbed 25% from 2019 to 2020 and jumped to $92.9B in 2021, then swung up to $111.4B in 2022 and down to $99.6B in 2023 before recovering to $108.8B in 2024 and settling at $98.8B in 2025.
